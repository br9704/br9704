## About Me

I am a software engineer and UX designer studying a Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering and UX Design at the **University of Melbourne**, one of Australia's top-ranked universities for technology and design. I build at the intersection of product design and engineering, and I ship both halves of a product, the engineering depth and the design taste, as one person.

I take products from concept all the way to the App Store on my own, with an engineering-led process and a modern toolchain that lets me ship polished, production-grade work at the pace of a small team. My work spans iOS apps, full-stack web platforms, and agentic AI systems, and I bring a 3D and motion background from indie-game and personal work, with a particular interest in data visualisation and 3D interaction on the web.

- Two apps live on the App Store right now: **Sentinel** and **Poke AI**
- Currently building **Hive**, a shared brain for AI coding agents
- Placed **5th out of 183** projects at UNIHACK 2026 with **SpeechMAX**
- Based in Melbourne, Australia. Open to part-time engineering and design roles, and graduate roles.
